# Restaurant Taste Profile

A summary of the dining preferences behind this recs list — useful as a reference
when adding new places or researching recommendations.

## The one constant: veg/vegan-friendly

Every place on the list is vegetarian- or vegan-friendly. The underlying data
tags each as **Vegan**, **Veg** (vegetarian), or **Both** (full menu has solid
options for either). This is the non-negotiable filter — a place doesn't make the
list unless a veg/vegan diner eats well there.

- Fully **vegan** spots: Cha-Ya, Shizen, Wildseed, Golden Era, aíso, Om Sabor,
  Sassy Foods, The Lucky Creation, Next Level Burger.
- Strong **veg** (vegetarian) spots: Udupi Palace, Tartine, Kahnfections,
  U:Dessert Story.
- Most of the rest are **Both** — omnivore-friendly but reliably great for
  veg/vegan.

## Cuisine range — broad and globally curious

Taste is wide, not narrow. The list spans:

- **Japanese / ramen** — Cha-Ya, Shizen, Mensho Tokyo, Marufuku, Menya Kanemaru
- **Indian / South Indian** — Udupi Palace, Diwali
- **Mexican / Latin American** — Otra, Al Carajo, Loló, Donaji, El Buen Comer,
  Flores, Nopalito, Om Sabor; plus Venezuelan arepas (Pica Pica, Arepas Latin),
  Peruvian (Mochica, La Mar), Argentine (Lolinda), Bolivian (Peña Pachamama)
- **Italian / pizza** — Flour + Water, Gusto Pinsa, Joyride (Detroit-style),
  Tony's, Montesacro, Outta Sight, CyBelle's, Fiorella-adjacent
- **Mediterranean / Middle Eastern** — Oren's Hummus, Freekeh, Boochmania, Savor,
  Moya (Ethiopian)
- **Chinese** — Mamahuhu, Dumpling Kitchen, Sassy Foods, The Lucky Creation
- **Vietnamese** — Golden Era
- **Californian / tasting menus** — Greens, Nightbird, aíso

## A serious dessert habit

Desserts are a category unto themselves on this list — roughly a fifth of the
"rec" places. Heavy loyalty to a few names:

- **Dandelion Chocolate** — collects locations (Valencia flagship, 16th St
  Factory, Ferry Building, Chestnut St)
- **Tartine** — Bakery flagship + Manufactory
- Plus Garden Creamery, Koolfi Creamery (Indian ice cream), Elaichi Co. (chai),
  Kahnfections, U:Dessert Story

Takeaway: ice cream, chocolate, bakeries, and chai/dessert cafes all count.
Multiple outposts of a beloved brand are welcome, not redundant.

## Price & vibe

- Mostly **$ to $$** — casual, neighborhood, everyday-good places.
- A few **$$$/$$$$** splurges reserved for occasions: Flour + Water, Nightbird
  ($$$$ tasting), Lolinda, La Mar, Greens.
- Format runs from counter-service and dumpling shops to sit-down and tasting
  menus — quality matters more than formality.

## Geography

Centered on the **east/central** side of the city — Mission (the densest
cluster), SoMa, Hayes Valley, North Beach, Cow Hollow/Marina, Castro/Noe Valley,
Inner Sunset/Richmond. Lighter coverage out west and south.

## rec vs explore

- **rec** ("Exploit" in the source data) — tried and endorsed; safe to recommend.
- **explore** — personal wishlist, not yet visited / not publicly endorsed.

## How to use this when adding places

A new place is a strong fit when it:

1. Has real veg/vegan options (ideally tag it Vegan / Veg / Both).
2. Falls into — or thoughtfully extends — the cuisines above.
3. Is genuinely good for its category, not just convenient.
4. Counts double if it's an exceptional dessert/bakery/ice-cream/chai spot.
